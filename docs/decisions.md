# Decisions

## Made

1. Scope: professional liability only (beroepsaansprakelijkheid). Ten-year liability and general third-party liability are out.
2. Pilot segment: architects and interior architects first.
3. KBO is used for sizing, triggers and companies. Not for outreach to sole traders.
4. Website and register matches use confidence levels (verified, probable, weak). Probable matches are used for outreach; weak ones need manual review. Changed 19 Oct 2026: a wrong match costs less than missing prospects.
5. Qualification of unknown leads finishes in the first conversation, not in the pipeline.
6. Channels: e-mail first, WhatsApp only after a reply or a click on the WhatsApp link.
